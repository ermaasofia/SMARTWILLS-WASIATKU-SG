What SmartFaraid?

Understand Your Estate Distribution

SmartFaraid is an Islamic inheritance preparation and Faraid planning service that helps Muslim families in Singapore identify rightful heirs and understand the estimated share of each heir according to Faraid principles.

The service includes reviewing family information, listing assets and liabilities, identifying eligible heirs, and preparing an initial overview of estate distribution before the estate administration process begins.

SmartFaraid helps you understand :

  • Who is Faraid's rightful heir?
  • Assets included in the estate
  • Debts and liabilities that need to be settled
  • Estimated share of each heir
  • Preparation of documents before inheritance matters begin

SmartFaraid is more than a calculation — it is an early preparation to help families understand the complete estate position clearly.

Importance of Faraid

Reduce Confusion, Simplify Estate Preparation

Identify Rightful Heirs

Make it easier for families to know who is eligible to receive inheritance according to faraid law.

Organize Asset and Debt Information

Gather important asset and liability details so the estate administration process can be handled more systematically.

Estimated Distribution of Inheritance

Provide an early overview of the possible share each heir may receive.

Without proper preparation, families may face:

Unidentified Heirs

Difficulty identifying who is eligible to receive the estate.

Outstanding Assets or Liabilities

Incomplete Document

Missing or incomplete important documents can delay the process.

Misunderstanding in Distribution

Unclear information may lead to misunderstanding in estate distribution.

Delayed Estate Administration

The process becomes long and complicated, delaying the solution.

Family Disputes

Differences of opinion can affect family relationships.

Clearer preparation helps estate administration begin in a more organised way and reduces the burden on heirs.
