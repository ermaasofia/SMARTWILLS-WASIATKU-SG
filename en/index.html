What Is an Islamic Wasiat?

An Islamic Wasiat is a valid will document for Muslims in Singapore that allows you to state your wishes and appoint selected executors, beneficiaries, including individuals or charitable organisations, to receive your assets after you pass away.

It helps ensure your estate planning is carried out clearly, responsibly, and in accordance with Shariah principles.

Importance of Wasiat

A Wasiat is not only an important responsibility for Muslims in Singapore, but also an act of worship that helps protect your loved ones and ensure your wishes are carried out according to Shariah principles.

Still Have Questions? ›
01

Why do I need a Wasiat?

A wasiat allows the testator to record how their assets should be distributed after death, alongside distribution under Faraid.

Without a wasiat, appointing an executor and managing the estate can become complicated, time-consuming and costly.

02

Can I appoint SmartWriter as a witness?

Yes, you can appoint us as a witness.

However, WasiatKu reserves the right to charge up to a maximum of SGD 100 per witness for each wasiat processed.

03

Do you need a lawyer to prepare a wasiat?

In Singapore, the law does not require a wasiat to be prepared by a lawyer.

A wasiat is considered valid and enforceable when it is in writing, properly signed, and meets the relevant legal requirements.

04

Are online wasiat valid?

Our online wasiat is a legally binding document drafted by our panel of lawyers and reviewed by in-house legal counsel to ensure compliance with Singapore law and Shariah principles.
