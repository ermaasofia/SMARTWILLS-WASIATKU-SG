SmartWills WasiatKu

SmartWills WasiatKu is a digital will preparation platform that helps individuals prepare a will in a more convenient, safe, affordable and accessible way at any time.

Who We Are?

We are a financial technology company committed to meeting today’s growing demand for digital financial services that are simple, fast, secure, and trusted.

SmartWills WasiatKu is developed through the collaboration of financial advisors, estate planners, legal professionals, and technology experts. It was created to provide an easier, more affordable, and accessible Will writing service for individuals from different financial backgrounds. At the end of 2016, we began our initiative to develop a user-friendly digital Will writing platform that could be accessed through both website and mobile application. After nearly three years of careful planning, market research, and software development, we proudly launched our online Will writing service in Malaysia in early 2019.

To meet the growing needs of our clients, we expanded our presence by opening a branch in Singapore in mid-2023. Today, SmartWills WasiatKu continues to provide a trusted digital Will preparation solution designed for modern users who value convenience, security, and peace of mind.
